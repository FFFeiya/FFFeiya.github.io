Player’s Manual for Be A Vtuber!

✨ Welcome to the World of Virtual Streamers!

Hey there, friend behind the screen! Welcome to the party of Be A Vtuber! – the ultimate virtual streaming experience! You’ll step into the shoes of a virtual streamer, using your talents and wisdom to dominate the streaming world, defeat your competitors, and climb to the top as the “Rank1V” (国一V)!

Here, you have just one goal: Gain followers, make money, and reach the pinnacle of fame!

Friendly Tip: If your friends suddenly start gaining popularity, don’t get jealous — it means they’re truly immersed in the game!

☑ Game Setup

  1. Character Selection
    Each player picks a virtual streamer character card, learning the character’s starting skills and stats. Grab your character token and get ready to “pose”!

  1. Determine the Number of Rounds
    The game lasts for 6 rounds, each representing one month of a streamer’s career. You can extend or shorten the number of rounds depending on how much time everyone’s willing to invest!

  2. Prepare the Decks
    Shuffle the event cards, activity cards, and other components according to the rules, and arrange them neatly on the table. Chaos on the table may lead to complaints from your fans!

📣 Gameplay Flow

The Beginning of Each Month

  1. Choose Your Theme
    Each player selects a number card as their live streaming theme. Reveal your cards to see who is the “idea genius” for the month!

  2. Draw Your Starting Cards
    Each player draws 8 cards from their number deck. Discard one card immediately and place it face up as the discard pile for everyone to admire.

Weekly Process

  1. Plan Your Strategy
    Every week, play two cards from your hand — one for the “left hand” and one for the “right hand” — to decide your streaming content.

  2. PK Mode Begins

    • Players challenge each other in PK battles, using their “right hand” cards to compete against the “left hand” cards of the player to their right.
    • PK Rules:
      • If the difference in numbers ≤ 3: The player with the higher number wins, and their traffic = the difference in numbers.
      • If the difference in numbers > 3: The player with the lower number wins, and their traffic = the difference in numbers.
      • If the numbers are equal: Both players receive 1 point of traffic.

  3. Draw Event Cards to Boost Popularity
    Draw event cards every round and use your strategy to engage in exciting activities, attracting more fans and tips!

  4. Traffic Becomes Followers
    At the end of each month, tally up your traffic to calculate your followers. Remember, followers = your source of income, so attracting fans is key!

The End of Each Month

  1. Event Card Phase
    Draw the top card from the event deck, triggering a special event. It might bring you fortune or misfortune — luck plays a big part here!

  2. New “Rank1V”
    The player with the most followers at the end of the month becomes the new “Rank1V”! They get to go first in the next month!

🔹 Game Components Explanation

  • Character Cards
    Represent the virtual streamer you’re playing as, displaying their basic stats: “Chat Power,” “Singing Power,” “Gaming Power,” and “Dance Power.” Check out the image to see the unique features of your streamer!

  • Follower Tracker
    Keeps track of each player’s follower count. Followers are your future hope! (See the image)

  • Earnings Cards
    100 earnings cards are divided into categories like “Light Stick,” “Captain,” “Admiral,” and “Governor.” The more you earn, the higher your status!

  • Event Cards
    Various cards for live streaming events that trigger different effects to help you gain followers and increase earnings. (See the image)

⚔ Victory Conditions

After 6 months of streaming, the player with the most earnings wins! When you reach the top, don’t forget to shout with your friends: “I’m the Rank1V!”

⚡ Pro Tips

  1. Don’t Forget About Your Skills!
    Each character has passive skills and special cards that can help you turn the tide!

  2. Adapt to Events!
    Unexpected events could drastically change the game, so stay prepared for surprises!

  3. Making Money is Key!
    Don’t just chase fame; earning profits is the real game changer!

  4. Play the Friendship Card!
    Collaborative events can make fans go wild with excitement, so don’t forget to team up when needed.

Start your streamer journey now! Follow the streamer meow, follow the streamer thank you meow!
