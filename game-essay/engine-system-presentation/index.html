Joint presentation: an AI state machine library and a collision system

Feiyang Yang and Benjamin Yang present their engine systems for Game Engineering II: what each one does, how a game uses it, what was tricky, and how the two compare and could work together.

What the presentation covers

  1. Both systems in one sentence each, and why we present them together.
  2. The AI state machine library: what it does, a live demo in MyGame, how a classmate adds it to their game, how it uses what we learned this semester, and something that was tricky.
  3. The collision system: the same questions, answered by Benjamin.
  4. A comparison of the two systems, and a sketch of how they could work together in one game.
